What We Do

Games

Engaging, innovative, and diverse games built in Unity. We started on mobile and are expanding toward PC, VR, and consoles.

Developer Tools

High-quality Unity tools that streamline game creation, including in-editor task management and versatile character controllers.

Online Play

A multiplayer platform with matchmaking, leaderboards, achievements, and tournaments is in active development to power our upcoming titles.

Who We Are

Eternity Game Studios was founded by a self-taught developer with a deep passion for game development. What began as a way to sharpen technical skills became a love for creating immersive experiences, and years of building mobile games in Unity.

Our mission is to craft games that captivate and inspire. We believe games are a powerful form of art, capable of telling unique stories and providing endless entertainment and educational value. Alongside our games, we build and sell tools that help fellow developers ship their own projects faster.
